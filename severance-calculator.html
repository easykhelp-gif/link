📌 Korean Labor Law Guidelines for Severance Pay

  • Minimum Qualification: Continuous service for at least 1 full year (365 days) working 15+ hours per week.
  • Statutory Deadline: Payment must be remitted within 14 calendar days of resignation.
  • Legal Formula: Average Daily Wage × 30 days × (Total Service Days ÷ 365).
