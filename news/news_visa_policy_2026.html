Korea Visa & Immigration Policy Updates 2026

Updated: August 2026 · Kori Care Information Portal

📌 Executive News Summary

The Ministry of Justice of South Korea has released vital immigration policy changes aimed at supporting long-term foreign residents and skilled workers in 2026.
  • E-9 to E-7-4 Transition Quota: Expanded allocation for foreign workers with 4+ years of continuous legal employment in Korea.
  • F-2-7 Residency Points: Bonus evaluation points awarded for higher KIIP level completion and vocational language skills.
  • Online Alien Registration: Initial ARC applications, address changes, and stay extensions can now be processed online via HiKorea.
