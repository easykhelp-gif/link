Foreign Worker Rights & Severance Pay Protection

Updated: August 2026 · Kori Care Information Portal

📌 Executive News Summary

Under Korean Labor Standards Act, ALL foreign workers who have completed 1 year of continuous employment are legally entitled to severance pay regardless of visa type or company size.
  • 1-Year Employment Rule: Working 15+ hours per week for 1 continuous year guarantees 30 days of average salary for each year served.
  • 14-Day Payment Statutory Deadline: Employers must remit full severance pay within 14 calendar days of resignation or contract end.
  • 20% Interest Penalty for Delays: Employers face legal action and a mandatory 20% annual interest penalty on overdue payouts.
🌐 Official Source: Ministry of Employment and Labor (MOEL) ➔
