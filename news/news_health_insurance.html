Healthcare Enrollment Guide

National Health Insurance Access for Foreigners

Updated: August 2026 · Kori Care Information Portal

📌 Executive News Summary

Foreign residents living in South Korea for more than 6 months are legally required to enroll in the National Health Insurance Service (NHIS).
  • Automatic Enrollment: Registration takes effect automatically after 6 months of continuous residency in South Korea.
  • Comprehensive Medical Benefits: Grants 70-80% discount coverage for outpatient hospital visits, clinics, and prescription drugs.
  • Multilingual Support Center: NHIS provides dedicated assistance in English, Chinese, and Vietnamese via 033-811-2000.
🌐 Official Source: National Health Insurance Service (NHIS) ➔
