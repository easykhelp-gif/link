Seoraksan in One Day — Cable Car, Autumn Colours and Sokcho (2026)

Seoraksan is the first place in Korea to turn red each autumn, and a cable car lifts you to a 700-metre lookout without a hike. This guide is how to get there from Seoul, what one day costs, and the ticket mistakes that add hours or waste the trip.

When to go

Autumn colour reaches Seoraksan first. For 2026, the first leaves are forecast to turn in late September to early October, with the peak in mid-to-late October — roughly 16 to 25 October.

The colour starts at the top and works its way down. The valley near the park entrance turns later than the peaks, so a visit in late October catches more of the lower slopes.

In winter the ridges hold snow, and for anyone from Thailand or Vietnam that is a reason on its own to come back.

Getting there from Seoul — buy the right bus

There is no train to Sokcho. Searching for a KTX will waste your time. Buses are the only way — and the ticket you buy matters more than the terminal you leave from.

At Dong Seoul, check that the ticket says express (고속) or non-stop (직통). The intercity bus to Sokcho leaves from the same building and takes about an hour and a half longer.

If you need more departure times to choose from, the Seoul Express Bus Terminal runs 54 buses a day.

From Sokcho to the mountain

From Sokcho terminal, take city bus 7 or 7-1 to Seorak-dong Sogongwon (소공원), the main park entrance.

  • Fare 1,700 KRW in cash, 1,530 KRW with a transport card
  • About 45 minutes
  • Every 17 to 20 minutes during the day

The cable car

The Seorak Cable Car runs from Sogongwon up to Gwongeumseong (권금성) at about 700 metres. The ride takes about 5 minutes each way.

You cannot book ahead. There is no reservation system at all — the weather changes too often, so tickets are sold on the day at the counter only. On autumn weekends that means arriving early.

Tickets are sold as round trips only. When you get off at the top, there is still a short walk uphill to the fortress viewpoint. It is not a hike, but it is not flat either.

Adult (middle school and up)16,000 KRW

Operating hours change with the season and the weather, and the times for each day are posted on the operator's homepage. Call 033-636-4300 before you travel.

Entry to Seoraksan National Park costs nothing, and since May 2023 neither does the temple inside it. Before that, visitors paid a temple fee just to use the trails.

After the mountain — Sokcho

Sogongwon and Sokcho city are close enough to do both in one day. Take bus 7 or 7-1 back into town.

Sokcho Tourist and Fishery Market (속초 관광수산시장) is the food stop in the city centre — the place most visitors head to eat.

Then the sea. Sokcho beach and the rocky lookout at Yeonggeumjeong (영금정) are both in the city, so you can finish the day facing the East Sea instead of rushing for a bus.

Mountain, market and sea in one day is the reason this trip works.

Abai Village — where Autumn in My Heart was filmed

Abai Village (아바이마을) sits across the harbour channel, and the way over is the gaetbae (갯배) — a flat ferry that the passengers themselves pull across the channel along a steel cable. The crossing takes about 5 minutes and costs 500 KRW for an adult, 300 KRW for a child. The landing is a short walk from the market.

The village is where Autumn in My Heart (가을동화, 2000) was filmed — the drama often credited with starting the Korean Wave. Vietnamese viewers know it as *Trái tim mùa thu*, Thai viewers as *รักนี้ชั่วนิรันดร์*.

If the cable car is not running

It happens often. Wind and weather stop it, and the decision is made on the day.

This does not end the trip. The valley path from Sogongwon is gentle and walkable without gear, and the temple grounds are on level ground.

One thing to know: the valley sits far lower than the peaks, so it turns later. On the peak weekend the slopes above may be fully red while the valley is still changing. By late October the colour reaches the bottom.

Check first: 033-636-4300.

Questions people ask

Is there a train to Sokcho?

No. Sokcho has no railway station, so a KTX search will return nothing useful. Express buses from Seoul are the way in.

How long does it take from Seoul?

About 2 hours on the non-stop express bus from Dong Seoul Terminal, or about 2 hours 35 minutes from the Seoul Express Bus Terminal. The intercity bus from Dong Seoul takes about 3 hours 30 minutes — check your ticket before you board.

Can I book the Seoraksan cable car in advance?

No. There is no advance booking. Tickets are sold on the day at the counter, because the weather decides whether it runs at all. Arrive early on autumn weekends.

How much is entry to Seoraksan National Park?

Nothing. Park entry is free, and the Sinheungsa temple fee was abolished in May 2023. You only pay for the cable car if you take it.

Where was Autumn in My Heart filmed?

In Abai Village (아바이마을) in Sokcho. You reach it on the gaetbae ferry — 500 KRW for an adult, about 5 minutes — a short walk from Sokcho Tourist and Fishery Market.
