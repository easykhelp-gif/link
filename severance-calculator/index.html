Korean Labor Standards Act Regulations

  • Qualification: Continuous service at the same workplace for 1+ year (365 days) working 15+ hours weekly.
  • Statutory Deadline: Full severance must be remitted within 14 calendar days of resignation.
  • Late Penalty: Unpaid balances past 14 days accrue 20% annual interest penalty by law.
